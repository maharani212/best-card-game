# Starfall Duel

An original cosmic card battler: the Star Captain duels the Nebula Warden with a 20-card deck of original cards. Each turn grants energy to cast damage, shields and healing; first side to 0 life loses. Playable offline in a desktop browser from `game/index.html` — no build step, no network.

## How to play

- Both sides start at 30 life. Reduce the Warden to 0 before yours runs out.
- Each turn you gain energy (3, then +1 per turn, max 10) and draw a card.
- Click a card to cast it if you can afford its cost; press END TURN and the Warden answers.
- Shields block damage but fade at the start of your next turn.
